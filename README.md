## 📌 About the Project

As Generative AI rapidly transforms industries, many working professionals experience what we term **"Algorithmic Anxiety"** — the stress that arises from the fear of being replaced by AI, coupled with a sense of skill obsolescence.

This qualitative research project investigates whether cultivating a **growth mindset** can serve as an effective psychological tool to:

- 🛡️ **Mitigate workplace stress** caused by AI integration
- 🔄 **Counteract perceived skill obsolescence** in rapidly evolving roles
- 💪 **Prevent emotional exhaustion** among professionals adapting to change
- 📚 **Foster continuous learning** as a sustainable career strategy

> *"The illiterate of the 21st century will not be those who cannot read and write, but those who cannot learn, unlearn, and relearn."* — Alvin Toffler

---

## 🔬 Research Methodology

| Aspect | Details |
|:---|:---|
| **Approach** | Qualitative Research |
| **Method** | Semi-structured Interviews |
| **Participants** | Working professionals across diverse domains |
| **Analysis** | Thematic Analysis of interview transcripts |
| **Framework** | Growth Mindset (Carol Dweck) × AI Adoption Stress |

### 🎯 Key Research Questions

1. How do working professionals perceive the impact of Generative AI on their job roles and skill relevance?
2. What coping mechanisms do professionals employ to manage AI-induced workplace stress?
3. Does a growth mindset orientation correlate with greater adaptability and resilience to AI disruption?
4. What role does continuous learning play in mitigating feelings of skill obsolescence?
